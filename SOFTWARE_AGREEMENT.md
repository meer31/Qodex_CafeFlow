### 1. SOFTWARE DETAILS & CORE SCOPE
**Software Name:** **Qodex CafeFlow (Version 1.0.0)**  
**Type:** Dedicated Desktop/Mobile Restaurant POS & Management Solution  
**Key Modules Included:**
* **POS Billing & Invoicing:** Fast counter order processing, customizable receipts, and thermal printer integration.
* **Table & Dine-In Management:** Floor layouts, table status tracking, and waiter assignment.
* **Product & Category Catalog:** Menu items, prices, modifiers, and category controls.
* **Recipe & Inventory Tracking:** Ingredient linking with automated stock deduction upon sales.
* **Raw Material & Meat Purchase:** Supplier purchase recording and cost monitoring.
* **Expense Tracker & Financial Reporting:** Daily/monthly expense logs, revenue analytics, and profit/loss breakdown.
* **Executive Dashboard:** Live metrics, active orders, and sales performance graphs.

### 2. LOCAL DATABASE & DATA AUTONOMY
* **Database Engine:** Standalone Local SQLite Database (`cafe_flow.db`).
* **100% Offline Capability:** Operates fully without requiring an active internet connection. Zero downtime caused by internet outages.
* **Data Privacy & Ownership:** 100% of data (sales, recipes, financial metrics, customer logs) is stored locally on the client's premises. No third-party data harvesting or unauthorized remote access.
* **Zero Recurring DB Hosting Fees:** No monthly or annual cloud database hosting subscriptions required for daily operations.
* **Backup & Restore:** Client maintains complete control over local database backups and storage archives.
