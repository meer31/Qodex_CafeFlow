2. Software Identity & Included Scope

The Provider licenses Qodex CafeFlow (Version 1.0.0), tailored for daily restaurant & cafe workflows:

  • POS Counter Billing: Fast order entry, item search, discounts & thermal receipt printing.
  • Table & Dine-In Management: Real-time floor status, dine-in tables, and waiter assignment.
  • Menu & Category Management: Full catalog management with pricing & variations.
  • Recipe & Stock Automation: Automated ingredient depletion based on dish recipes.
  • Raw Material & Meat Purchase: Supplier cost tracking & raw inventory reception.
  • Expense & Financial Tracking: Daily expenses, profit/loss calculations & comprehensive reports.
  • Sales History & Void Audits: Complete invoice log, bill reprinting & refund tracking.
  • Executive Dashboard: Real-time revenue cards, active tables & sales charts.
3. Local Database Architecture & Data Ownership
Standalone Local SQLite Database (cafe_flow.db):
  • 100% Offline Autonomy: Operates entirely without internet connection. Outages will never disrupt counter billing.
  • Complete Data Privacy: All transaction records, recipes, prices, and revenue logs remain 100% on the client’s local computer. No cloud leakage or external hosting risk.
  • Zero Recurring Database Costs: No monthly or annual cloud subscription charges required to store or access your data.
